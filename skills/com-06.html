Storytelling

Communication COM-06

Detailed description

Storytelling is the art of telling a story to get a message across. Our brain remembers a narrative with characters and emotions far better than a list of arguments: nobody remembers a table of figures, but everybody remembers a good tale.

An effective story follows a simple structure: a hero (very often the client, not the brand), an obstacle to overcome, then a transformation. The brand plays the role of the guide who helps the hero. Be careful: a brand story must stay authentic. Inventing facts destroys public trust as soon as it is discovered.

Examples of use cases

  • An agency’s “About” page that starts with “It all began in a garage, with two second-hand computers…”.
  • A case study told as a story: the client’s starting situation, their problem, the solution found, the result.
  • A pitch to a potential client that opens with an anecdote rather than a list of services.

Vocabulary to know

Pitch
A very short, punchy presentation of an idea or a company.
Case study
A detailed account of a project carried out for a client, with its results.
