SEO

Marketing MKT-01

Detailed description

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization, in French référencement naturel. It is the set of techniques that make a website appear in a good position in Google’s results without paying. When you type a question into Google, the first results are not there by chance: they were judged the most useful.

SEO rests on three pillars. Content: answering precisely the questions people ask. Technical quality: a fast, well-structured website adapted to mobile. Popularity: receiving links from other websites, a bit like recommendations. SEO is a marathon: results often come after 3 to 6 months, and Google regularly changes its rules.

Examples of use cases

  • Writing an article “How much does a showcase website cost?” to appear when people ask that question.
  • Optimizing the titles and descriptions of every page so that they make people want to click.
  • A Lyon plumber takes care of their local Google listing to appear for “plumber Lyon” (local SEO).
  • Getting a link from a professional directory or a press article.

Vocabulary to know

Keyword
The word or phrase people type into Google.
Backlink
A link from another website to yours.
SERP
The search engine results page.
