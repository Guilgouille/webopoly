Crisis Management

Communication COM-08

Detailed description

A communication crisis is an event that endangers reputation: a bad buzz on social networks, an outage, a mistake, a controversy, a data leak. On the Internet everything moves very fast: within a few hours, bad news can be seen by thousands of people.

The principles of good crisis management are simple to state and hard to apply under pressure: detect quickly (thanks to monitoring), react quickly, tell the truth, admit your mistake, offer a solution and appoint a single spokesperson. Never delete criticism and never reply in the heat of the moment. The best reflex is to prepare beforehand: a crisis plan with clear roles and messages approved in advance.

Examples of use cases

  • A client’s website goes down on sales day: the agency warns people, apologizes, gives a repair deadline and keeps its promise.
  • A clumsy post by the agency triggers a controversy: it publishes a clear apology and explains what is changing.
  • A data leak: the people concerned are informed, and in some cases the law also requires notifying the authority (the CNIL in France) within 72 hours.

Vocabulary to know

Bad buzz
A wave of negative reactions on the Internet.
Monitoring
Watching what is said about the brand.
Spokesperson
The person who speaks on behalf of the company.
