Brand Platform

Graphic Design DES-08

Detailed description

The brand platform is the foundation of identity: it answers the question “who are we, and why?”. It defines the mission (why the company exists), the vision (where it wants to go), the values (the principles that guide its choices), the personality (how you would describe it if it were a person) and the tone of voice (how it speaks: informal or formal address, humor or seriousness).

You can compare it to the foundations of a house: the logo, the colors, the communication and the website all flow from it. It is often built in a workshop with the team and the clients. One essential point: values are useless if they change no decision. “Transparency” only means something if the agency really agrees to give a client bad news.

Examples of use cases

  • Choosing three clear values, such as “boldness, transparency, simplicity”, and referring to them to settle decisions.
  • Setting the tone of voice: informal and light humor for a sports brand, formal and sober for a law firm.
  • Checking that a logo and a color palette really match the defined personality.

Vocabulary to know

Mission, vision, values
Why we exist, where we are going, what guides us.
Tone of voice
The brand’s way of expressing itself.
Brand personality
The character traits attributed to it.
