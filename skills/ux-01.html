Persona

UX/UI Design UX-01

Detailed description

A persona is the portrait of a typical user, fictional but realistic, built from real data: interviews, questionnaires, statistics. It is given a first name, an age, a job, goals, frustrations and digital habits (which device, how comfortable with the web).

Its value is that it lets the whole team design for someone specific rather than for “everyone”: a site designed for everyone ends up suiting nobody. You generally create two to four personas. Beware of the trap: a persona invented from clichés is worse than no persona at all. It must rely on real observations.

Examples of use cases

  • “Julie, 34, bakery manager, short on time, mostly uses her smartphone”: so you choose big buttons and very short forms.
  • In a meeting, the team asks “Would Julie understand this button?” to settle a disagreement.
  • For a site aimed at seniors, a persona who is not comfortable with technology leads to larger text and simpler navigation.

Vocabulary to know

Target
The group of people you create for.
Pain point
A difficulty or frustration of the user.
User interview
A one-to-one conversation to understand needs.
