Positioning

Marketing MKT-08

Detailed description

Positioning is the place the agency wants to occupy in its clients’ minds. It answers the question: “why us rather than a competitor?”. If everyone does “a bit of everything”, nobody remembers you; by choosing a precise field, you become the reference for those who need it.

A solid positioning rests on three elements: a precise target, a promise (the benefit delivered) and a proof (what makes the promise credible). You can sum it up in one sentence: “For [the target], we are the [category] that [the promise], because [the proof].” Positioning maps, which place competitors on two axes such as price and quality, help spot the empty spaces. Positioning then guides everything else: the logo, the messages, the website.

Examples of use cases

  • “The web agency specialized in restaurants” rather than “an agency that does everything”.
  • Choosing a high-end positioning (few clients, high prices) or an accessible one (many clients, simple offers).
  • An agency that positions itself on eco-design: lightweight websites that use little energy.

Vocabulary to know

Value proposition
What the company offers that is valuable to its clients.
Differentiation
What makes the offer different from competitors’.
Target
The group of clients you aim at.
