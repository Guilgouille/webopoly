Online Advertising (SEA)

Marketing MKT-04

Detailed description

SEA stands for Search Engine Advertising: paying to appear at the top of Google’s results (the links marked “Ad” or “Sponsored”) or in social network feeds. The most common mechanism is an auction: the advertiser chooses keywords, sets a budget, and generally pays only when an internet user clicks on the ad.

Its advantage: results are immediate and highly targeted (you choose age, city, interests). Its drawback: everything stops as soon as the budget stops, unlike SEO. You therefore have to track the return on investment and test several versions of an ad. An ad leads to a landing page designed to make the visitor take action.

Examples of use cases

  • A Google Ads campaign on “web agency Lyon” to receive quote requests.
  • An Instagram ad promoting a shop’s sales to 25-35 year-olds in its region.
  • Retargeting: showing an ad to visitors who looked at a product without buying it.

Vocabulary to know

CPC (cost per click)
The price paid for each click.
Landing page
The page where the visitor arrives after clicking.
ROI (return on investment)
What the advertising brings in compared with what it costs.
