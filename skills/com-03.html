Community Management

Communication COM-03

Detailed description

The community manager is the person who runs a brand’s online community. Where the “Social Media” skill is about publishing, this one is about conversation: answering comments, private messages and reviews, thanking people, reassuring them, calming tensions. The community manager is the human face of the brand.

This job requires responsiveness (a reply within 24 hours is a good benchmark), a tone that matches the brand’s identity, and a cool head when facing criticism. It also acts as a sensor: by reading customers’ comments, the community manager spots recurring problems and passes them on to the teams.

Examples of use cases

  • Replying politely, and with a solution, to a negative Google review.
  • Answering a question asked in the comments under a post (“Do you deliver to Belgium?”).
  • Moderating: deleting abusive comments or spam while leaving well-argued criticism.
  • Noticing that ten customers are complaining about the same bug and reporting it to the technical team.

Vocabulary to know

Moderation
Monitoring and filtering the content posted by the community.
DM (direct message)
A private message exchanged with a user.
Monitoring
Regularly keeping an eye on what is being said about the brand.
