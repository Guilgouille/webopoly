Social Media

Communication COM-01

Detailed description

Social networks (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube…) are platforms where anyone can publish content and react to other people’s posts. For a web agency they play three roles at once: a showcase (showing what it can do), a way to attract clients and a reputation tool.

Each network has its own audience and its own codes. LinkedIn speaks to professionals and accepts a serious tone. Instagram is very visual: you post polished images. TikTok relies on short, spontaneous videos, watched mostly by young people. The classic beginner’s mistake is trying to be everywhere: you wear yourself out and publish mediocre content. It is better to choose one or two networks where your clients really are, and post there regularly.

Examples of use cases

  • An agency targeting companies posts case studies on LinkedIn, such as “How we doubled the visits to a law firm’s website”.
  • An agency working with restaurants shows “before / after” pictures of redesigned websites on Instagram.
  • An agency looking for work-study students posts behind-the-scenes videos on TikTok to make people want to join.

Vocabulary to know

Algorithm
The program that decides which posts are shown to which people.
Engagement rate
The share of people who react to a post (like, comment, share).
