User Testing

UX/UI Design UX-07

Detailed description

A user test means watching real people use a website or a prototype. You give them a task (“Find the price of this offer”) without helping them, and you note where they hesitate, make mistakes or get lost. They are often asked to “think aloud” so you can understand their reasoning. The fundamental principle: you are testing the product, not the person. If they make a mistake, the site is badly designed.

According to a famous study by researcher Jakob Nielsen, five testers are enough to spot about 85% of the most common usability problems, which makes the method accessible even on a small budget. Tests can be done in person or remotely by video call. They work better when repeated during a project than when done once at the end.

Examples of use cases

  • Asking 5 people to find the price of an offer and noting where they hesitate.
  • Having people try a mobile prototype to check that the contact button is easy to find.
  • Discovering that nobody notices the “Confirm” button because it is too discreet, and making it stronger.

Vocabulary to know

Test scenario
The mission given to the tester.
Think aloud
Saying what you are thinking while using the site.
Usability (ergonomics)
The ease and comfort of using a product.
