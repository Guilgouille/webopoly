Automated Emailing

Marketing MKT-05

Detailed description

Automated emailing sends the right message at the right time, with no human intervention. You prepare “scenarios” once and for all that are triggered by an action or a date. The principle is an “if… then…”: if someone signs up, then they receive a welcome email; if a customer abandons their cart for 24 hours, then they receive a reminder.

For this to work, you segment your contacts (you sort them into groups according to their behavior) and personalize the messages. The advantage: you can look after thousands of people with the same finesse as ten. The limit: too many emails tire people and drive them away. The rules on consent and unsubscribing remain mandatory.

Examples of use cases

  • A welcome email sent right after signing up to the newsletter.
  • An abandoned-cart reminder, sometimes with a small promo code.
  • A follow-up email sent to customers who have bought nothing for three months.

Vocabulary to know

Scenario (workflow)
The sequence of emails planned for a specific case.
Trigger
The event that starts the scenario.
Segmentation
Splitting contacts into groups.
