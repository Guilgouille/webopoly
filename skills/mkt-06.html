Influencer Marketing

Marketing MKT-06

Detailed description

Influencer marketing means working with content creators (influencers) who have a loyal community, so that they present or recommend a brand. It works because their opinion seems more sincere than an advertisement: we trust someone we have followed for a long time.

We distinguish micro-influencers (a few thousand followers, but very close to their audience) from the big stars (several million followers). The right choice does not depend only on the number of followers: the community’s engagement and how well it matches the target matter more. In France, the law also requires making it clear that a post is a commercial collaboration.

Examples of use cases

  • A tech micro-influencer presents the new website of a start-up client.
  • A cosmetics brand sends its products to content creators so that they can test them.
  • You give the influencer a personal promo code, which lets you measure the sales they bring in.

Vocabulary to know

Micro-influencer
A creator with a small but highly engaged community.
Engagement rate
The share of the community that reacts to posts.
Commercial collaboration
A post made in return for payment or a benefit, which must be disclosed.
