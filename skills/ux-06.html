Interactive Prototype

UX/UI Design UX-06

Detailed description

An interactive prototype links mockups together to simulate navigation: you click a button and the next page appears, as on a real website, even though not a single line of code has been written yet. It is the equivalent of a model car you can already try out to feel how it reacts.

Its main benefit is financial: fixing a problem on a prototype costs far less than once development is finished. It is used to have the client approve the journey, to present it to investors and above all to have real users test it. It is made with tools such as Figma or ProtoPie, with more or less realism depending on the need.

Examples of use cases

  • Having the client test the ordering journey before development starts.
  • Presenting to investors an application that does not exist yet, but that they can already “use”.
  • Detecting that an important button goes unnoticed by letting a few people try the prototype.

Vocabulary to know

Interaction
The user’s action and the interface’s response.
Transition
The visual effect that accompanies the move from one screen to another.
Fidelity
The degree of resemblance to the final product.
