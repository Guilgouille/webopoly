UI Mockup

UX/UI Design UX-05

Detailed description

The UI mockup is the final design of a page: colors, fonts, images, icons, buttons, spacing. It is the faithful image of what the user will see, like a photo of the finished page before it exists. It is most often made in a tool such as Figma, from the approved wireframe and following the brand guidelines.

A good mockup is designed for several screen sizes (computer, tablet, phone) and shows the different states of elements: a button in its normal, hovered and disabled states, a field with an error. We often advise starting with mobile (mobile first), because most visits come from smartphones. The mockup then becomes the developers’ working plan.

Examples of use cases

  • Delivering the home page in a desktop version and a mobile version, ready to be developed.
  • Designing a form with its states: empty, filled, error message, confirmation.
  • Having the client approve the mockup before development starts.

Vocabulary to know

UI
User Interface, the visual appearance and the elements people interact with.
Mobile first
Designing for the phone first, then adapting to large screens.
State of an element
Its appearance depending on the situation (normal, hovered, disabled).
