Brand Guidelines

Graphic Design DES-04

Detailed description

The brand guidelines (charte graphique in French) are the guide that gathers all of a brand’s visual rules. They include the logo (its versions, the space to leave around it, its minimum size, what never to do with it), the colors with their codes, the typefaces and their uses, the style of icons and photos, and examples of application on a business card, a website or a post.

Their purpose is consistency: everything the brand produces should look alike, even when several people, agencies or printers are involved. They are a bit like the rulebook of a game: without it, everyone plays their own way. They are usually delivered as a PDF of about twenty pages, together with the source files (logos, fonts, colors).

Examples of use cases

  • Handing the client a twenty-page PDF with all their logo files.
  • A new intern creates a poster that matches the brand’s identity simply by following the guidelines.
  • The printer finds the exact CMYK codes in the guidelines so that the colors are faithful.

Vocabulary to know

Clear space
The empty area to respect around the logo.
Variant
A version of an element (vertical logo, single-color version…).
Consistency
The fact that all materials look alike and are recognizable.
