User Journey

UX/UI Design UX-04

Detailed description

The user journey describes each step a person goes through to reach a goal: what they do, what they think, what they feel and the obstacles they meet. It is often presented as a timeline with an emotion curve: satisfaction, hesitation, annoyance.

Its real value is to reveal the friction points: the moments when the user gets stuck, makes mistakes or gives up. These are precious opportunities for improvement, because this is exactly where clients are lost. It naturally goes with a persona: you follow “Julie” step by step.

Examples of use cases

  • Describing the whole path, from the Google search to sending a quote request.
  • Mapping a purchase journey: discover, compare, add to cart, pay, receive.
  • Noticing that having to create an account before paying drives many buyers away, and offering guest checkout.

Vocabulary to know

Friction point
An obstacle that bothers or discourages the user.
Touchpoint
A moment of contact between the user and the brand.
Drop-off
Leaving a journey before the end.
