E-commerce Website

Development DEV-04

Detailed description

An e-commerce site lets you sell online. It brings together several building blocks: a catalog of products with their pages (photos, descriptions, prices), a cart, a checkout flow (the steps up to purchase: address, delivery, payment), secure payment, then the management of orders, stock, deliveries and invoices. Payment generally goes through a specialized provider: the site does not keep credit card numbers itself.

You can choose a ready-made solution (Shopify) or a module built into a CMS (WooCommerce, PrestaShop). What matters above all is trust and simplicity: a visitor only buys if they feel reassured (reviews, terms and conditions of sale, return policy, secure payment) and if buying is easy. Every extra step before payment loses customers.

Examples of use cases

  • Creating the online shop of a jewelry designer with product pages and card payment.
  • Offering guest checkout (no account needed) to reduce abandonment.
  • Automatically sending an order confirmation email with the summary and delivery tracking.

Vocabulary to know

Checkout flow
The series of steps leading from the cart to payment.
Terms and conditions of sale (CGV in French)
Mandatory on a commercial website.
Abandoned cart
A cart that was filled but never paid for.
