Typography

Graphic Design DES-03

Detailed description

Typography is the choice of fonts and the way they are set. It affects both readability (can you read it easily?) and the brand’s personality (is it serious, playful, luxurious?). We distinguish serif fonts (with small strokes at the ends of the letters), which suggest the classic look of books, from sans-serif fonts, which look more modern and clean.

The basic rule is never to use more than two fonts: one for headings, which can have character, and a very readable one for body text. You then create a hierarchy with sizes, weights (bold, light) and spacing to guide the reader’s eye. Finally, watch the license: a font that is free for personal use is not always free for a commercial project.

Examples of use cases

  • Pairing an expressive font for headings with a very readable sans-serif for paragraphs.
  • Defining a hierarchy: main title large and bold, subheadings smaller, body text at a comfortable size.
  • Checking a font’s license before adding it to a client’s website.

Vocabulary to know

Serif
A small decorative stroke at the end of letters.
Typographic hierarchy
The organization of sizes and styles that shows what is important.
Line spacing (leading)
The space between two lines of text.
