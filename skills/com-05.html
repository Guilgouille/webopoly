Press Relations

Communication COM-05

Detailed description

Press relations means building links with journalists and media so that they talk about the agency on their own initiative. Unlike an advertisement, which you pay for, an article is written by a third party, so readers give it more credibility.

To interest a journalist you need real news: a launch, an award, a study with figures, a testimonial. The basic tool is the press release, a short text that announces something by answering “who, what, when, where, why”. Journalists receive hundreds of messages a day, so you must target the right media and personalize the message.

Examples of use cases

  • Sending a press release to the local press when the agency wins a design award.
  • Offering a regional newspaper the testimonial of a client whose business was transformed by their new website.
  • Offering yourself as an expert to comment on the news, for example when a new accessibility rule comes into force.

Vocabulary to know

Press release
A short text announcing news to the media.
Press kit
A more complete presentation document about the company.
Press coverage
The articles and mentions obtained.
