Events

Communication COM-07

Detailed description

Event marketing means organizing events, or taking part in them, to meet your audience in person: trade shows, conferences, client evenings, workshops, webinars (online events). Nothing replaces a handshake for building trust, and big contracts are often born during a meeting.

A successful event is prepared: you set a goal (meeting 20 prospects, for example), a budget, a date and a place, you invite the right people and take care of the logistics. The most forgotten step is the follow-up after the event: if you do not contact people in the days that follow, all the effort is lost. Finally, you must measure the return on investment, because an event costs time and money.

Examples of use cases

  • Having a stand at a digital trade show and handing out business cards.
  • Organizing an afterwork evening with clients to strengthen the relationship.
  • Hosting a free webinar “Understanding SEO in 45 minutes” to collect contacts.

Vocabulary to know

Networking
Building a network of professional contacts.
Lead
A contact likely to become a client.
Webinar
A conference broadcast online.
