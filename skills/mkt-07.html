Growth Hacking

Marketing MKT-07

Detailed description

Growth hacking is a mindset: making a company grow fast, with few resources, by experimenting constantly. The cycle is always the same: you have an idea, you test it on a small scale, you measure the result, then you keep what works and drop the rest.

It mixes marketing, data and a little technical know-how. A famous example is Dropbox, which offered free storage space to those who referred a friend: every user became a small salesperson. It is not a magic formula: you already need a good product and reliable data. A failed test is not a failure, it is information.

Examples of use cases

  • Offering a free month of maintenance to every client who recommends the agency to a friend (referral).
  • Testing two different titles on a page to see which pushes more visitors to act.
  • Adding a well-placed share button and checking whether the number of visitors increases.

Vocabulary to know

A/B test
Comparing two versions of the same element to keep the better one.
Funnel
The steps a visitor goes through until purchase, with losses at each step.
Virality
When users themselves spread the product around them.
