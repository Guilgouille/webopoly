Logo

Graphic Design DES-01

Detailed description

The logo is a brand’s visual signature: a symbol, a piece of text, or both together. Its role is to be recognized at a glance, a bit like recognizing a face in a crowd. We distinguish the wordmark (the brand name drawn as a design, like Google’s), the symbol (an image alone) and the combination logo (symbol and name).

A good logo is simple and memorable. It must also be adaptable: readable in color or in black and white, tiny (the icon in a browser tab) or huge (a shop sign), on a screen or on a T-shirt. To achieve this, it is drawn as a vector image: a picture described by mathematical shapes that can be enlarged endlessly without becoming blurry, unlike a photo made of pixels. Several versions are usually delivered: horizontal, vertical, single-color, light background and dark background.

Examples of use cases

  • Testing the logo in miniature (16 pixels wide) to check that it stays recognizable in the browser tab.
  • Giving the client a version for light backgrounds and one for dark backgrounds, so they can use it everywhere.
  • Simplifying an overly detailed logo that becomes illegible in the small round frame of a social media profile picture.

Vocabulary to know

Vector
An image format that does not lose quality when enlarged (SVG, AI).
Wordmark
A logo made only of the brand name.
Favicon
The tiny icon shown in the browser tab.
