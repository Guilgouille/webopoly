Market Research

Marketing MKT-02

Detailed description

Market research means collecting and analyzing information before launching: who are the clients and what do they want? Who are the competitors and what do they offer? What are the trends? It avoids the classic trap: creating an offer nobody wants, or entering a market that is already saturated.

We distinguish quantitative data (figures, obtained for example through a questionnaire) from qualitative data (opinions and motivations, obtained through interviews). You can use information that already exists (public studies, statistics, Google Trends) or produce your own. In the end, the research helps you decide: go or no go, with which offer and at what price.

Examples of use cases

  • Comparing the prices and services of the 10 web agencies in your city.
  • Sending a questionnaire to 50 shopkeepers: “Do you have a website? How much would you be willing to pay?”
  • Conducting 10 interviews with potential clients to understand their difficulties.
  • Checking with Google Trends that demand for a product is rising before setting up an online shop.

Vocabulary to know

Target
The group of clients you aim at.
Direct / indirect competitor
A company that sells the same thing / that meets the same need in a different way.
Benchmark
Comparing your practices with those of others.
