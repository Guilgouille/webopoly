Analytics

Marketing MKT-03

Detailed description

Analytics is the measurement of what visitors do on a website: how many come, from where (Google, social networks, direct access), which pages they read, how long they stay, on which device, and above all whether they take the expected action (buying, filling in a form). That action is called a conversion.

To measure, you add a small piece of code to the site, provided by a tool such as Google Analytics or Matomo. Because these tools set cookies, the law requires asking visitors for consent (the banner you see on almost every website). The central idea is to decide with facts rather than gut feeling. And above all not to drown in numbers: it is better to follow 3 or 4 key indicators than 50 curves.

Examples of use cases

  • Discovering that 70% of visitors use a phone and deciding to optimize the mobile display first.
  • Noticing that many visitors leave the payment page, and looking for the reason.
  • Comparing two advertising campaigns to keep the one that brings the most sales.

Vocabulary to know

KPI
Key performance indicator, the figure you follow to know whether it works.
Conversion rate
The share of visitors who take the expected action.
Bounce rate
The share of visitors who leave after seeing a single page.
