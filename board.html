The board

40 squares, from the student bedroom to La Défense tower. Pawns start on Start of the month and move clockwise. Prices rise the further you go. Tap a number on the board to see what that square does.

Square types

What each kind of square does when you land on it. Full details in the rules.

  • Office × 22

    Buy it if it is free. Owned by a rival? Pay their invoice. The colour band shows its group.

  • Hosting provider × 4

    Costs €200. Rivals pay €25, €50 or €100 depending on how many you own. Own all 4 to win.

  • Skill × 8

    Draw a Skill card and keep it in your hand. It’s free!

  • AI Lab × 1

    Draw an AI card: a bonus or a penalty, applied right away.

  • Tax × 2

    New computer: pay €200. Software subscriptions: pay €100.

  • Start of the month × 1

    Collect €200 each time you pass or land here.

  • Bug in prod × 1

    Just visiting, or stuck until you pay €50 or roll a double.

  • Your site crashes! × 1

    Go straight to Bug in prod.
