The 40 skills

Every Skill card stands for a skill that a web agency has to be able to call on. Each page explains one skill the way a teacher would in class: a detailed description, examples of use cases and the vocabulary professionals use. The identifier on each page (for example COM-01) is the one printed on the card.

Communication

Communication is the art of making the agency known and liked: showing your work, talking to the right audience, maintaining relationships and protecting your reputation.

Communication

Social Media

Social networks (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube…) are platforms where anyone can publish content and react to other people’s posts.

 Communication

Newsletter

A newsletter is an email sent regularly to a list of people who agreed to receive it.

 Communication

Community Management

The community manager is the person who runs a brand’s online community.

 Communication

Content Strategy

A content strategy is a plan that answers five questions.

 Communication

Press Relations

Press relations means building links with journalists and media so that they talk about the agency on their own initiative.

 Communication

Storytelling

Storytelling is the art of telling a story to get a message across.

 Communication

Events

Event marketing means organizing events, or taking part in them, to meet your audience in person.

 Communication

Crisis Management

A communication crisis is an event that endangers reputation.

Marketing

Marketing is the set of methods for understanding the market, attracting clients and making them want to buy, relying on figures as much as possible.

Marketing

SEO

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization, in French référencement naturel.

 Marketing

Market Research

Market research means collecting and analyzing information before launching.

 Marketing

Analytics

Analytics is the measurement of what visitors do on a website.

 Marketing

Online Advertising (SEA)

SEA stands for Search Engine Advertising.

 Marketing

Automated Emailing

Automated emailing sends the right message at the right time, with no human intervention.

 Marketing

Influencer Marketing

Influencer marketing means working with content creators (influencers) who have a loyal community, so that they present or recommend a brand.

 Marketing

Growth Hacking

Growth hacking is a mindset.

 Marketing

Positioning

Positioning is the place the agency wants to occupy in its clients’ minds.

Graphic Design

Design is a brand’s visual identity: what people see and recognize. Good design is not just pretty, it is also consistent, readable and faithful to the brand’s personality.

Graphic Design

Logo

The logo is a brand’s visual signature.

 Graphic Design

Color Palette

The palette is the set of 3 to 5 colors chosen to represent the brand and used everywhere, from the website to posters.

 Graphic Design

Typography

Typography is the choice of fonts and the way they are set.

 Graphic Design

Brand Guidelines

The brand guidelines (charte graphique in French) are the guide that gathers all of a brand’s visual rules.

 Graphic Design

Iconography

Iconography covers a brand’s icons (small pictograms like the magnifying glass or the shopping cart) and illustrations.

 Graphic Design

Print Materials

“Print” materials are printed documents.

 Graphic Design

Motion Design

Motion design means animating graphic elements.

 Graphic Design

Brand Platform

The brand platform is the foundation of identity.

UX/UI Design

UX (User Experience) is the experience lived by the user: is it easy, logical, pleasant? UI (User Interface) is the interface, what you see and touch. Think of a house: UX is the floor plan and how you move between rooms; UI is the paint, the furniture and the door handles.

UX/UI Design

Persona

A persona is the portrait of a typical user, fictional but realistic, built from real data.

 UX/UI Design

Site Map

The site map (information architecture) is the plan of all of a website’s pages and the links between them, like a tree.

 UX/UI Design

Wireframe

A wireframe is the skeleton of a page.

 UX/UI Design

User Journey

The user journey describes each step a person goes through to reach a goal.

 UX/UI Design

UI Mockup

The UI mockup is the final design of a page.

 UX/UI Design

Interactive Prototype

An interactive prototype links mockups together to simulate navigation.

 UX/UI Design

User Testing

A user test means watching real people use a website or a prototype.

 UX/UI Design

Design System

A design system is a library of reusable components (buttons, form fields, cards, menus…) together with their usage rules.

Development

Development means turning mockups into a site that really works: one that displays everywhere, loads quickly, can be used by everyone and protects its visitors’ data.

Development

HTML/CSS Integration

Integration means turning a mockup into a real web page that any browser can display.

 Development

Responsive Design

A responsive site automatically adapts to the screen size.

 Development

CMS Website

A CMS (Content Management System) is software that lets you create and manage a website without writing all the code by hand.

 Development

E-commerce Website

An e-commerce site lets you sell online.

 Development

Accessibility

An accessible site can be used by everyone, including people with disabilities.

 Development

Web Performance

Web performance measures a site’s speed.

 Development

Security

Security means protecting the site and its users’ data against hacking, information theft and outages.

 Development

Back-end

The back-end is the invisible part of a site.
