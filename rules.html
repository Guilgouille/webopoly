Setting up

Everything you need before the first roll.

Contents

1 game board · 26 title deeds (22 offices, 4 hosting providers) · 40 Skill cards · 10 AI cards · 5 Profile cards · 5 pawns · 2 dice · 250 banknotes · 1 add-on

Profiles and their family

  • Communication Officer→ Communication
  • Marketer→ Marketing
  • Graphic Designer→ Graphic Design
  • UI/UX Designer→ UX/UI Design
  • Developer→ Development

Your profile gives you half price when you place Skill cards from its family.

  1. Choose a profile, taking turns. Two players cannot have the same one.
  2. Take your starting Skill card: pick one at random from the 8 cards in your profile's skill category and read it aloud.
  3. Receive €1,500 (see the banknotes below). The rest of the banknotes form the bank.
  4. Make 2 separate decks, face down: Skill cards and AI cards, in their spots in the middle of the board.
  5. Place the pawns on Start of the month. The youngest player starts, then play goes clockwise.

Each player starts with

  • 2 ×€500
  • 2 ×€100
  • 2 ×€50
  • 6 ×€20
  • 5 ×€10
  • 5 ×€5
  • 5 ×€1

= €1,500

How to play

Who goes first? The youngest player starts, then play goes clockwise.

On your turn

  1. Roll the 2 dice and move your pawn that many squares.
  2. Do what your square says: buy an office, pay an invoice, draw a card… (see Types of squares below).
  3. If you want: place Skill cards on your offices, start an auction or make a deal.
  4. Rolled a double? Play again. Third double in a row: go straight to Bug in prod.

Start of the month

Each time you pass or land on Start of the month, collect €200 from the bank. It's your agency's monthly income!

Your profile

Your profile (Communication Officer, Marketer, Graphic Designer, UI/UX Designer or Developer) gives you a starting Skill card and half price when you place Skill cards from its family.

Types of squares

What happens when your pawn lands on each square.

1) Offices

Free? You can buy it at the price on the deed (read its sentence out loud). Owned by a rival? Pay their invoice. Your own? Nothing happens.

2) Hosting providers

Free? Buy it for €200. Owned by a rival? Pay €25 for 1 provider, €50 for 2, €100 for 3. Own all 4 and you win! See the providers.

3) Skill squares

Draw a Skill card, read it out loud and keep it in your hand. It's free! Scan its QR code to learn more in the app, or read it in the Skills section.

4) AI Lab

Pick an AI card and apply it right away: a bonus (the AI helps) or a penalty (it hallucinates, copies or leaks data).

5) Taxes

New computer: pay €200. Software subscriptions: pay €100. The money goes to the bank.

6) Your site crashes!

Go straight to Bug in prod, without passing Start of the month.

7) Bug in prod

Just visiting? Nothing happens. Sent there? Pay €50 or roll a double to get out. After 3 turns, pay €50 and leave. You still collect your invoices.

Good to know

  • If there are no Skill cards in your area: opposing players pay you the normal price on the bill.
  • 2 Skill cards from the same family: place them on 2 offices, auction one or trade it.
  • Office on hold (token on its square): you pay nothing.
  • Skill deck empty: the Skill square pays €50 instead.
  • Owner in Bug in prod: they still collect their invoice.
  • Hosting provider: Skill cards change nothing there.
  • Colour split between two players: nobody gets the doubled base invoice: negotiate.
  • Forgot to claim an invoice? Claim it before the next player rolls.

Don't wait for your turn!

Some actions happen outside your turn. Stay alert!

1) Collect your invoice

When a rival lands on your office, they pay you: base invoice + the value of every Skill card placed on it. The amounts are printed on the deed. Own the whole colour? The base invoice is doubled.

Forgot to ask? You can claim it until the next player rolls the dice.

2) Auctions

Put a Skill card from your hand up for auction: announce a starting price, the others bid, the highest bidder pays you and takes the card. It's the only way to buy a Skill card.

3) Place Skill cards

On your turn, place Skill cards on your offices: one per family, 5 max per office. Pay the bank the placement cost printed on the deed (half price for your profile's family).

4) Make deals with your rivals!

Between two turns, trade or sell offices, hosting providers, Skill cards in hand and the AI Debug card, for money or anything else. The price is up for negotiation.

Nobody wants your Skill card? The bank buys it for €50.

How to win

The first player to reach one of these goals wins straight away.

A full side

Own every office on one side of the board (hosting providers do not count).

All 4 providers

Own the 4 hosting providers.

Last one standing

All the other players have gone bankrupt.

One-hour variant: if nobody has won after one hour, the player with the most money wins.

The 10 AI cards

Drawn on the AI Lab square and applied right away. After use, put the card back at the bottom of the deck. Only AI Debug can be kept.

  • BonusAI Copywriter: draw a Skill card.
  • BonusAI Coding Assistant: place a Skill card for free.
  • BonusAI Chatbot: collect €100 from the bank.
  • BonusAI Image Generator: each player pays you €20.
  • BonusAI Debug: keep it: get out of Bug in prod for free.
  • PenaltyHallucination: pay €100 to the bank.
  • PenaltyPlagiarism: discard a Skill card, or pay €50.
  • PenaltyData leak: pay €150 to the bank.
  • PenaltyAI-generated bug: go straight to Bug in prod.
  • PenaltyAI tool outage: move back 3 squares.

Key takeaway: AI is a powerful tool, but always check what it produces.

Help! I need money!

If you have to pay more than you have:

  1. Remove placed Skill cards: the bank refunds half of their placement cost.
  2. Sell to another player.

Still can't pay? You're bankrupt: everything goes to the player you owe (or the bank) and you leave the game.
